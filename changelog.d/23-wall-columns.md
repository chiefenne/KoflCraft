- Polyhedral ducts: the wall layers now follow the Voronoi core cells (wall columns, `research/m4-wall-columns.md`). Each core cell's wall polygon in a wall triangle is extruded through the layers at fixed barycentric coordinates, and the polygons of one core cell over coplanar wall triangles merge into one column. Over curved walls no layer cell spans two triangles, because FoehnCraft fans a bent face about its node average. FoehnCraft reads both fixtures as valid with exact volumes and no inverted faces. Max non-orthogonality is 80.1° on the elbow (was 85.9°, faces above 70° 44 → 2) and 82.8° on SolidCraft (was 87.7°, 605 → 67). SolidCraft has 162,435 cells instead of 92,058, and sliver layer cells remain on its curved walls (smallest cell about 5e-11). `"wall_join": "split_cap"` in `volume_scaffold` keeps the earlier join. The hand-off merges a cell's boundary faces on one patch only when the result is flat. `mesh.estimate` counts two layer cells per wall triangle and layer for columns and labels `cells_basis` approximate. The Gmsh export writes each wall column as its native prisms. A Clippy lint of the current stable toolchain in `mesh-acceptance` is fixed.
