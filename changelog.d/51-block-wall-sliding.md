- Block mesher (MB1f): `block.grid` with `{"kind": "elliptic", "wall_sliding": true}` lets the wall nodes of curved extruded sections, block vertices included, slide along the section toward orthogonal grid lines. Sharp corners such as trailing edges stay fixed, and each node stays within half a cell of its TFI position. On the NACA 2412 C-grid, FoehnCraft's max non-orthogonality drops from 47.0° to 44.7°. Pipe and elbow walls do not slide yet.
