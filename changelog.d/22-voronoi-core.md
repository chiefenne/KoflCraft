- Polyhedral duct core (`volume_clipped_voronoi_cvt_v1`, now the default): a Voronoi diagram restricted to the scaffold tetrahedra, relaxed by ten density-weighted Lloyd iterations (Yan, Wang, Lévy & Liu). Elbow 1,355 cells instead of 7,005, SolidCraft 92,058 instead of 417,457. Both stay valid in `foehn mesh`. Max non-orthogonality is 85.9° and 87.7° (was 87.0° and 89.6°). SolidCraft faces above 70° drop from 3,038 to 605. Core sphericity p05 rises to 0.78 (elbow) and 0.81 (SolidCraft). At reentrant wall edges, sites are split into mirrored pairs and the diagram is rebuilt. A repaired cell keeps one face per neighbour, slivers are merged, and a layout FoehnCraft would reject is rebuilt with one more Lloyd iteration (`retries`). `"core": "median_dual"` in `volume_scaffold` keeps the old core. `lloyd_iterations` sets the relaxation, at most 200. The hand-off now merges the boundary faces one cell has on one patch, and the Gmsh export splits polygons with more than four nodes. `mesh.estimate` counts the Voronoi core, and `cells_basis` notes the cells added at reentrant edges.
