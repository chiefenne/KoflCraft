# Recipes

Short command sequences for everyday tasks. Pictures of complete examples
are in the [Example gallery](gallery.md); the tools and their inputs are
listed in [tools.md](tools.md).

The recipes are split by topic; each page has its own table of contents.
A recipe's number is the pull request that added its feature, the same as
its gallery entry.

| Page | What it covers | Recipes |
|---|---|---|
| [Block-structured meshes](recipes/block.md) | mesh a template, smooth with wall sliding, edit a topology, split flat corners, look at the blocks coloured and exploded in MurmelCraft | 37, 44b, 48, 71, 76 |
| [Polyhedral and layered meshes](recipes/polyhedral.md) | mesh a bare STL, name walls, choose how layers end, propose patches and a FoehnCraft skeleton, refine locally, add baffles and plates | 30, 57, 60, 62, 64, 70 |
